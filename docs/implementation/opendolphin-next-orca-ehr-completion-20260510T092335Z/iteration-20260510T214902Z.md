## Scope

- Assets: `chart_revision`, `chart_revision_event`, `chart_document.current_revision_id`, finalize REST route, document integrity contract.
- Trust boundary: client-provided patient, encounter, department, physician, insurance, finalizer, and content context is untrusted until server validation. Client-provided digest is not accepted as authority.
- Attack surface: incomplete context finalization, finalizing a revision under the wrong chart document, re-finalizing non-DRAFT revisions, and hiding context or raw patient details in event summaries.

## Misuse Cases

1. A client omits patient/encounter/department/physician/insurance/finalizer/content context and attempts to finalize anyway.
2. A client sends a `revisionId` that belongs to another `chart_document` or another facility.
3. A client tries to finalize an already FINAL revision or supply content that bypasses server-side hash calculation.
