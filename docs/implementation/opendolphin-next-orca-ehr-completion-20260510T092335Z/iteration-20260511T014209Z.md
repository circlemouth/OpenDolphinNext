## Misuse Cases And Mitigations

- Misuse case: candidate discovery alone is used to authorize billing/report live validation. Mitigation: dry-run requires exact selected-candidate preflight and rejects discovery-only readiness.
- Misuse case: raw patient/insurance details or auth/browser artifacts leak into evidence. Mitigation: output hashes patient identity and lists only sanitized accepted/forbidden evidence classes; direct leak scan verifies fixture details are absent.
- Misuse case: live ORCA traffic is triggered by the dry-run harness. Mitigation: command rejects live/artifact flags and reports `liveTrialOrca.executed=false`.
- Misuse case: uploaded report binary status is treated as paid/authoritative billing. Mitigation: claim boundary states the profile proves only ORCA-derived cache/snapshot acquisition and sanitized storage metadata.
