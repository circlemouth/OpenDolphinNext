## Scope

- Queue: A-09 missing ORCA acceptance blocks normal billing send.
- Checklist: 3.3, 6, 13.
- Assets: `encounter_projection`, server-derived ORCA受付 ID/time, `close-and-send-to-billing`, ORCA `medicalmodv2` transport boundary.
- Trust boundary: billing send context is server-side `encounter_projection`; client-provided patient, facility, voucher, sequential, insurance, classCode, URL, or raw XML cannot supply missing ORCA acceptance authority.
- Attack surface: sending ORCA billing without a valid acceptance, forging voucher/sequential in client payload, falling through to patient/karte/transport lookup with incomplete context, leaking patient or ORCA payload details in failure evidence.

## Misuse Cases

- A chart without ORCA acceptance is sent to ORCA because the client supplies voucher/sequential values.
- A facility-mismatched encounter supplies another facility's acceptance identifiers.
- Missing acceptance falls through to ORCA transport and creates ambiguous `UNKNOWN` state.
- Failure evidence records patient detail, insurance detail, raw XML, URL, or credential-like data.
