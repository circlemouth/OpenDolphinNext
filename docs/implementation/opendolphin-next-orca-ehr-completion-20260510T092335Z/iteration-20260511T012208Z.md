## Misuse Cases And Mitigations

- Misuse case: patient ID or UI-selected row is treated as sufficient live billing/report authority. Mitigation: profile requires same-RUN exact selected-candidate preflight and server-side context.
- Misuse case: raw invoice number, raw `Data_Id`, raw ORCA body, or report binary body enters release evidence. Mitigation: accepted evidence is limited to cache/snapshot hashes, counts, sanitized summaries, and server-generated storage metadata.
- Misuse case: `storageUploadStatus=UPLOADED` is interpreted as billing paid, receipt authoritative, or receipt/report source-of-truth. Mitigation: profile explicitly limits the claim to ORCA-derived cache/snapshot acquisition and staging.
- Misuse case: object storage upload failure is accepted as partial success. Mitigation: contract states enabled upload failures, digest mismatch, and retention gaps are fail-closed blockers.
